  installers to insert their own). I cannot tell from this repository
  whether the operator's record of processing activities, privacy policy or

  neither and says only essential cookies are used. The person declared the
  EU as a region where the product is offered. I assumed the operator is a
  controller, and I did not supply an establishment for either vendor. I
