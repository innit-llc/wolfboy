- What the review said: The privacy policy shipped at /privacy
  (src/server\.ts:13) promises that closing an account deletes the member's
  personal data within 30 days. The only close-account route
  (src/account\.ts:19) only sets closed \= 1 on the member row. It never
  deletes it. I searched the whole tree (excluding \.legal/) for DELETE
  FROM, purge and retention: nothing deletes members rows or runs after 30
  days. The members table (src/db\.ts) keeps name, e-mail, password hash
  and avatar URL, and the closed flag is read only to block log-in. So the
  stated erasure within 30 days has no code behind it in this repository.
  This is the promised-but-not-implemented direction of the
  policy-versus-code comparison. The policy line is the finding here and is
  the same processing as the account\.ts route. The product is offered in
