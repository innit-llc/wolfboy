<summary>The review's reasoning, in full</summary>

- What the review said: The repository supplies a container image: the
  Dockerfile builds and runs the Express server, and INSTALL\.md:16 tells
  each club to run it on its own server. INSTALL\.md:6-8 says Example Club
  Software GmbH sells it to clubs in the EU on a yearly licence. That is a
  product supplied for use in the course of a commercial activity. The
  person declared the EU and that this is a commercial product, and the
  scan's applicability verdict is likely-applies. 11 exclusion questions
  are still open, such as medical or defence use, and nothing in the code
  suggests any of them. I assumed the maker is a manufacturer, not an
  open-source steward, because it sells a licensed product under its own
  name. I looked in the whole tree for SECURITY\.md, security\.txt, a
  \.github folder and the words security, vulnerab and report. The tree is
  Dockerfile, INSTALL\.md, PRIVACY\.md, package\.json, public/ and src/. I
  found no disclosure policy and no security contact. The only contacts are
  sales\@clubsoftware\.example\.com (INSTALL\.md:8) and
  privacy\@bookclub\.example\.com (PRIVACY\.md:24). Those are a sales
  address and a privacy address, not a route for vulnerability reports. I
  could not see an organisation-level \.github repository or a product
  website, so the policy could exist outside this repository. Both the
  policy and the address are missing from what ships with the product. The
  duty applies from 2027-12-11, so this is preparation for a dated
  obligation, nothing chargeable today.
