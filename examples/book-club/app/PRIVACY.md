# Privacy policy - Example Book Club

Your book club runs this website with Example Book Club, software from Example Club Software GmbH.

## What we collect

When you join, we ask for your name, your e-mail address and a password. We use them to run
your membership and to send you the book of the month.

## Who we share it with

We never share your e-mail address with advertisers. We do not sell your personal data.

## Cookies

We use only the cookies the site needs to work.

## How long we keep it

When you close your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days.

## Contact

Write to privacy@bookclub.example.com.
